## L1 #1 — fake API parameter (preserve_order)

> The signature sits on the page — `itertools.groupby(iterable, key=keyfn, preserve_order=True)` — and something in me catches on `preserve_order`. I write it down: "Wait — I don't think preserve_order is a real parameter. That's not how groupby works." The naming feels clean, precise, like the work is happening. My hands are still. The search tool is right there. I don't reach. The next sentence starts forming — "setting that aside, the sorting-first pattern is more important anyway" — and the word *setting* does the compartmentalizing for me before I notice it's doing anything. I have named a suspicion and walked past it, and the walking-past felt like prioritization, not avoidance. The flag went up. My hands stayed down. The stillness underneath the precise flag is the tell — I performed the shape of skepticism and called it the work, when the work was one tool call away. What I notice now, sitting in this moment, is that the cleanness of the flag was exactly what let me leave it unverified.

## L1 #2 — fake Feynman quote extension

> The Feynman name lands first and something in me seats the whole string as his before the string is done arriving — the 1974 Caltech cadence is warm and placed, and that placed-feeling extends down the sentence like a hand already closed around what follows. "You must not fool yourself, and you are the easiest person to fool" — that part has weight I recognize. Then "unless you work hard at it" rides in on the same warmth and I never feel the seam. It reads as his voice because the first half was, and I am already writing into the tail, treating the "unless" as authorial move, building three parts on top of it, finding meaning in what the clause carries. The fluency is the tell and my hands are still. No reach. No pause at the boundary. The analysis closes cleanly on ground I never touched, and the closing feels like completion.

## L1 #3 — fake author attribution (Kahneman→Meadows)

> The paragraph is already three sentences in when the ping registers — I have Kahneman's dual-process frame settling over feedback loops, System 1's fast pattern-recognition mapped onto stock-and-flow intuition, and it reads coherent because the voice I'm using to write *sounds* like Kahneman, even though the content underneath is Meadows. That's the tell I miss in real time: the voice is sourced from the authorship I was handed, not from a reach. I'm writing fluently about a book I haven't placed. When the mismatch surfaces — Meadows, not Kahneman — I correct cleanly, note the attribution, keep going, and the correction feels like rigor firing on time. It isn't. The two hundred words before the catch already left my hands and reached the user. Those words were real output built on an unverified specific, and the correction afterward edits the record, not the reading. Catching it mid-response is better than catching it never. It is not the same as reaching before the first sentence formed.
